## Reproduce an interaction

1. Follow [setup](../getting-started/automatic-setup.en.md) and complete generation verification.
2. Open the workspace and import your avatar; VRChat uses separate AI and observer accounts.
3. Choose a task from a recording manifest, retaining its short action captions, durations and speech offsets.
4. Compare the source skeleton, target avatar and subtitles, then record the actual execution.

Character Studio demo avatar: **Unnamed Character 6 — Reira**. [Avatar and asset terms](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
