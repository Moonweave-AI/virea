# Visual direction

The visual system extends VIREA's interface: Inter typography, charcoal `#151622`, silver-white text, lilac `#bdb8d1`, and dusty rose `#c9adb8`. Product recordings keep their original colors and complete viewport. Browser footage is 4K; VRChat footage keeps its native 2560 × 1494 proportions. Generated lunar illustrations provide brand atmosphere, not evidence of product output.

The shot vocabulary adapts video-shotcraft's **carousel-3d**, **steep-tilt-glide**, **dataviz-landscape-open**, **integration-hub-map** and **blur-slide** recipes. The carousel uses finite station changes; the side glide passes into a front-facing view; Set Up uses inverse-pose spatial flights. These are product-specific adaptations, not exact demo reproductions.

Text reveals combine position, opacity, blur and restrained rotation. Camera motion resolves into reading holds. Silver arc wipes connect chapters; the close retains the final statement and repository URL. Animation is frame-driven with fixed procedural patterns.

The opening illustration uses the supplied banner design and the anatomical explanation uses a generated full-body digital character. The 4K output preserves sharp UI footage; the banner artwork itself is 1774 × 887. No small UI crop is enlarged to impersonate a full-resolution capture.
